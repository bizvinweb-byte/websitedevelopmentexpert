## 1. Keyword Mapping Philosophy & Cannibalization Prevention

To avoid internal keyword cannibalization (where multiple pages on the same domain compete for identical search queries and dilute Google rankings), every high-intent keyword cluster is strictly mapped to **exactly one primary canonical landing page**.

### Cannibalization Audit & Separation of Intent:
- **Homepage (`/`)**: Focuses on the overarching brand entity (*Website Development Expert, a venture of Brandmingo*) and national high-authority broad inquiries (*"website development company in India"*, *"web development agency India"*).
- **National Delivery Hub (`/website-development-company-in-india`)**: Focuses specifically on nationwide remote engineering workflows, sprint delivery across Indian states, transparent timelines, and remote project execution.
- **Noida Headquarters Page (`/website-development-company-in-noida`)**: Captures local Noida and Sector 62 physical office inquiries (*"website development company in Noida"*, *"web designers in Sector 62 Noida"*, *"website company near me"*).
- **Service Pages (`/services/*`)**: Each captures a dedicated technology category (*Shopify*, *Next.js*, *E-Commerce*, *WordPress*, *Custom Web Apps*).
- **Cost Guides (`/website-development-cost-in-india`)**: Captures transactional cost, pricing tiers, and budget estimation searches.

> - Never send blast emails. Research the journalist, editor, or webmaster's recent articles and personalize the opening paragraph.
> - Send maximum 1 follow-up email 4 to 5 business days after the initial outreach. If no response is received, archive the prospect.
> - Send all outreach from an authentic `@websitedevelopmentexpert.in` corporate email address, never a free Gmail account.

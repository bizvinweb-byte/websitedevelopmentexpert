# Ethical Link Acquisition & Digital PR Strategy for Indian Web Engineering

> **GOOGLE SPAM POLICY & PENALTY WARNING:**
> Google's algorithms (including SpamBrain and Link Spam updates) aggressively detect and penalize artificial link schemes. 
> - **NEVER buy backlinks on Fiverr, private blog networks (PBNs), or link broker marketplaces.**
> - **NEVER engage in reciprocal link exchanges ("I link to you, you link to me") at scale.**
> - **NEVER publish spun or low-quality sponsored guest posts.**
> A single algorithmic or manual link penalty can de-index an entire commercial domain. Every backlink we target must be editorial, relevant to software engineering or Indian business, and earned through high-value technical assets.

## 3. Client Footer Credit Policy ("Powered by")

When engineering custom websites for clients, including a discreet footer credit is standard industry practice, but **must be implemented strictly in accordance with Google's webmaster guidelines to prevent unnatural link penalties**:

### Correct Implementation:
```html
<p class="text-xs text-slate-500">
  Website Engineered by 
  <a 
    href="https://websitedevelopmentexpert.in" 
    target="_blank" 
    rel="nofollow noopener" 
    class="hover:underline font-semibold"
  >
    Website Development Expert
  </a>
</p>
```

### Critical Rules:
1. Always include `rel="nofollow"` on sitewide footer credits. Sitewide exact-match anchor text without `nofollow` is a known trigger for Google Penguin/Spam algorithms.
2. Always use the branded anchor text (`Website Development Expert`), never commercial keywords (`Best Web Development Company`).
3. Only place credits on client sites with explicit client permission in the project contract.

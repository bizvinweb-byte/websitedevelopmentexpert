# Ethical Client Review Acquisition & Management Guide

> **STRICT COMPLIANCE & LEGAL NOTICE:**
> - **Never offer incentives (discounts, gift cards, cash) in exchange for reviews.** Google's review policies strictly prohibit incentivized reviews and will permanently remove listings that violate this rule.
> - **Never post fake, automated, or employee reviews.** Google actively deploys machine-learning filters that detect review velocity anomalies and IP clustering.
> - **All reviews must reflect authentic, verifiable client experiences.**

---

## 1. Timing the Review Request: The 3 Golden Windows

Clients are most receptive to leaving thoughtful, detailed reviews during key satisfaction moments:

1. **Window 1: Staging Milestone Approval (High Engagement):**
   When the client reviews and approves their interactive Figma designs or working Next.js/Shopify staging prototype.
2. **Window 2: Production Launch Day (Peak Excitement):**
   Immediately after the domain is pointed live, DNS propagates, and the first successful transaction/lead test is verified.
3. **Window 3: Post-Launch 30-Day Health Check (Validated ROI):**
   During the 30-day post-launch check-in call when real conversion metrics and positive business impact are confirmed.

## 2. Review Links: Quick-Access Formats

Use the official Google Business Profile short review link generated from your Google Business Profile manager dashboard:
- **Direct Review URL Format:** `https://g.page/r/[YOUR_UNIQUE_GBP_ID]/review` (Generate in GBP Manager > *Ask for reviews*)
- **Website Reviews Page:** `https://websitedevelopmentexpert.in/reviews` (Shows verified client endorsements with Schema.org markup)

## 4. Professional Review Reply Templates

Every review—positive or negative—must be professionally answered within 24 to 48 hours. Google values profile engagement and responsiveness.

### Reply to Positive Review (5 Stars)
```text
Thank you so much for the kind words, [Client Name]! It was a pleasure collaborating with the team at [Client Company Name] to engineer your [mention technology, e.g. custom Next.js web application / Shopify 2.0 storefront]. We take immense pride in delivering clean, scalable architectures from our Sector 62, Noida office. We look forward to supporting your platform's long-term growth!
```

### Reply to Constructive / Neutral Review (3–4 Stars)
```text
Thank you for your honest feedback, [Client Name]. We are delighted that the core website architecture and performance are delivering results for [Client Company Name]. We also appreciate your notes regarding [mention specific point, e.g. timeline communication], which we have shared with our project management team to refine our sprint updates. We remain committed to your success!
```

### Reply to Critical / Negative Review (1–2 Stars)
```text
Hello [Reviewer Name], thank you for bringing this to our attention. At Website Development Expert, we take engineering standards and client satisfaction with utmost seriousness. We would like to investigate this directly and ensure any outstanding issues are resolved immediately. Please reach out to our senior leadership at +91 99906 13140 or contact@websitedevelopmentexpert.in with your project details so we can assist you right away.
```
